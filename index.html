About our paint

Washable, non-toxic paint

We fill every kit with high-quality, washable, non-toxic paint so tiny artists can create freely and parents can enjoy the fun with less worry about cleanup. More colorful memories, less stress about the mess! 🎨

A family project

Our story

Three Tiny Brushes began when our oldest daughter was just six years old. She came to me with a sweet idea: she wanted to start a craft business with her two little sisters. We’ve always loved crafting together and making special things for the people we love, so dreaming up craft kits to share felt like a natural next step.

Together, we designed our logo with three tiny brushes—one for each of our girls, in their favorite color. Every kit is created and packaged by hand and holds a little piece of our family’s love of crafting, ready to bring joy to your tiny artist.
